SECTION E - SYSTEM ACCURACY AND TAMPER DETECTION

Initial problem:
The system needed controlled authentic, tampered, and unknown-document cases. Vector modifications were repeatedly difficult to interpret because PDF editors and re-saving can alter a document without an obvious visual change.

Fix and decision:
Vector/editor changes are treated as a possible modification. This is intentional: a document that has passed through an editor cannot be assumed unchanged merely because the visible text appears the same.

Results:
The controlled dataset was rerun after the vector-handling clarification. Authentic, text-tampered, image-tampered, vector-tampered, metadata-tampered, structurally changed, and unknown cases were evaluated without relying on filename-based demonstration results.
