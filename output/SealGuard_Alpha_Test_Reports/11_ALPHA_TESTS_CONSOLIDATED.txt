Initial project-wide problems:
- Role and Clerk-account permissions were unclear.
- Failed login attempts were not visible in the dashboard.
- Document duplicates, revisions, Trash, and audit details needed clearer lifecycle behavior.
- PDF viewing and downloading produced incorrect audit events in some paths.
- Mobile navigation, PDF viewers, filters, dropdowns, and horizontal overflow caused usability problems.
- Load testing exposed duplicate/revision races and limitations in the evidence harness.
- Vector/editor PDF changes were difficult to classify and required a conservative tamper policy.

Major corrective outcomes:
- Authentication, lockout, RBAC, private-document authorization, and audit logging were strengthened.
- Document validation, duplicate-aware revision prompts, metadata, version history, Trash, reports, and exports were expanded.
- Cryptographic, fingerprint, seal, tamper, and sensitive-data checks were exercised.
- Locust and reliability evidence were preserved with failures and limitations instead of being hidden.
- Responsive navigation and document/verification views were tested across desktop, tablet, iPad, Android, and deployed environments.
